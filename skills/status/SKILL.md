# Flight Safety Command status

Call `status` and tell the Owner, in a few short lines: departments with their Vertical Heads and hired agents, each
open work request with its progress, who is working or waiting to run, and the dashboard link (if it is running).
If agents are waiting to run, say `/flight-safety-agents:run` lets them work.
